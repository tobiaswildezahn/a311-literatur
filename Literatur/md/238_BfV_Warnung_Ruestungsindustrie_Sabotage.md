# Espionage, Sabotage, Attacks: German Intelligence Agency Sends Warning to Defence Industry

**Published:** 14/04/2026
**Author:** Franziska Müller

---

Germany's Federal Office for the Protection of the Constitution (BfV) has issued warnings to the country's defence industry regarding escalating espionage, sabotage, and cyberattack threats.

## Recent Cases

In March, two suspected Russian spies were arrested after allegedly stealing secrets from a German drone manufacturer. Another case involved a Ukrainian national accused of collecting military information for Russian intelligence services. Additionally, a Nuremberg businessman faced charges for supplying underwater surveillance technology to Russia through a European supply network.

## Scale of the Problem

According to a confidential Federal Criminal Police Office report, authorities documented 321 suspected sabotage cases in 2025, with particular concentrations in North Rhine-Westphalia and Lower Saxony.

## Industry Expansion and Vulnerability

Germany's defence sector is rapidly expanding, with companies like Rheinmetall and Quantum Systems increasing production capacity. Quantum Systems plans to deliver 10,000 drones to Ukraine. However, this growth has created vulnerabilities for newcomers unfamiliar with security protocols.

## Official Response

BfV President Sinan Selen stated: "Authoritarian states are increasingly using hybrid means...specifically against our economy, research institutions and critical infrastructures."

Germany adopted a national economic protection strategy in March and implemented new KRITIS legislation to protect critical infrastructure. The focus emphasizes resilience through cooperation between government and private sector actors.
