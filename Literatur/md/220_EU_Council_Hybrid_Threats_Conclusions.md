# Council Adopts Conclusions on Advancing the EU's Capacity to Counter Hybrid Threats

**European Council / Council of the European Union**
Press Release | 16 March 2026

## Main Approval

The Council approved conclusions on strengthening the EU's ability to address hybrid threats, emphasizing commitment to "prevent, deter and respond to hybrid campaigns" regardless of origin or scale.

## Key Condemnations

The Council condemned persistent hybrid operations by state and non-state actors targeting EU stability, specifically denouncing:
- Sabotage of critical infrastructure
- Malicious cyber activities
- Foreign information manipulation and interference
- Election interference
- Migration weaponization

Russia and its proxies received particular condemnation for "persistent, coordinated and long-standing hybrid campaigns" that undermine Ukraine's defense capabilities.

## Response Strategy

The Council reaffirmed using available instruments including the EU hybrid toolbox, cyber diplomacy toolbox, legislation, and restrictive measures. Priorities include:
- Increasing costs for those conducting hybrid operations
- Protecting critical infrastructure
- Defending democratic processes
- Supporting affected partner nations

## Background Definition

Hybrid threats represent "coordinated harmful activities planned and carried out with malign intent" designed to remain below war thresholds. Methods include information manipulation, cyberattacks, economic coercion, and military threats.

The EU established its hybrid toolbox following the March 2022 Strategic Compass for Security and Defence.
