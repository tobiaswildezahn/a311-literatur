# EU Preparedness Union Strategy to prevent and react to emerging threats and crises

**News article**
26 March 2025
Directorate-General for European Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid Operations (ECHO)
4 min read

---

## Overview

The European Commission and High Representative unveiled the Preparedness Union Strategy, designed to strengthen member nations and bolster Europe's capacity to manage emerging threats. The initiative responds to mounting geopolitical tensions, cyber threats, disinformation campaigns, climate emergencies, and natural disasters.

The comprehensive approach encompasses **30 priority actions** and a detailed implementation roadmap, emphasizing preparedness embedded throughout EU policies.

## Leadership Statements

President von der Leyen emphasized that "New realities require a new level of preparedness in Europe" and called for citizens and businesses to possess tools for both prevention and rapid response to disasters.

Commissioner Lahbib stressed that "Preparedness must be woven into the fabric of our societies — everyone has a role to play," highlighting the need for proactive, coordinated European-level responses.

## Core Strategy Objectives

**Essential Services Protection:**
- Establish baseline preparedness requirements for hospitals, schools, transportation, and communications
- Strengthen reserves of vital supplies
- Improve climate resilience and resource management

**Citizen Engagement:**
- Promote emergency readiness, including maintaining supplies for "a minimum of 72 hours"
- Integrate preparedness into school curricula and establish an EU Preparedness Day

**Crisis Management:**
- Create an EU Crisis Hub coordinating existing structures

**Military and Civil Coordination:**
- Conduct unified EU-wide preparedness drills
- Support dual-use technology investments

**Risk Assessment:**
- Build comprehensive EU-level threat evaluation systems

**Business Partnerships:**
- Establish a Preparedness Taskforce engaging industry
- Develop emergency protocols ensuring supply continuity

**International Engagement:**
- Strengthen cooperation with NATO on mobility, cyber defense, and industrial capabilities

## Strategic Foundation

The approach integrates an all-hazards framework combining governmental coordination across local, regional, national, and EU levels with whole-of-society participation from citizens, civil society, businesses, and academic institutions.
