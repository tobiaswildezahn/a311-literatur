# Stronger Protection of Critical Infrastructure

**Date:** Thursday, 29 January 2026
**Source:** Bundesregierung (bundesregierung.de)
**URL:** https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-en/news/cabinet-kritis-umbrella-law-2404992

---

## Overview

Germany's Bundestag has approved the Umbrella Act for Critical Infrastructure Protection (KRITIS), establishing uniform safeguard standards across multiple sectors. This legislation implements an EU directive aimed at enhancing system resilience.

## Sectors Covered

The law addresses nine critical sectors collectively for the first time: energy, transport, finance and insurance, health, drinking water, wastewater, waste disposal, IT and telecommunications, food, space, and public administration.

## Key Provisions

**Identification Standards**

The legislation defines critical systems nationwide. Facilities qualify when they're essential to overall German supply and serve over 500,000 people.

**Protection Requirements**

The act establishes cross-sector minimum standards for physical protection. Operators must implement measures such as "emergency teams, stronger property protection and resilience measures" based on government-prepared risk assessments and incident reporting obligations.

## Parliamentary Modifications

Amendments expanded federal states' authority to designate additional facilities under their jurisdiction. The Federal Ministry of the Interior gained power to establish criteria through statutory order requiring Bundesrat approval.

The government also committed to reviewing the act after two years rather than five, and will incorporate transparency considerations into a broader Resilience Strategy for Critical Infrastructure.
