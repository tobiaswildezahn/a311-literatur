# EU hosts high-level conference to mark 1 year of its Preparedness Union Strategy

## Overview

The European Union opened a landmark two-day conference in Brussels on March 23, 2026, to commemorate the first anniversary of its Preparedness Union Strategy. The event gathered government officials, civil protection specialists, military leaders, business executives, and European partners.

## Key Details

**Launch and Purpose**

The strategy was introduced in March 2025 to address evolving global challenges. The Commission framed it as responding to „a changing world in which geopolitical shifts, climate events and technological risks increasingly call for a holistic and coordinated European approach."

**Leadership**

The proceedings were opened by Executive Vice-President Roxana Mînzatu and Commissioner for Equality, Preparedness and Crisis Management Hadja Lahbib, alongside Oleksii Kuleba, Deputy Prime Minister for Restoration of Ukraine.

**Conference Focus**

Four thematic breakout sessions produced actionable recommendations. The event emphasised lessons from Russia's war against Ukraine and wildfire management challenges, preceding the adoption of a Communication on integrated wildfire risk management.

## Einordnung (A311)

Die EU Preparedness Union Strategy, vorgestellt im Maerz 2025 von der ehemaligen finnischen Staatspraesidentin Sauli Niinistoe (Niinistoe-Bericht) und Hadja Lahbib, bildet nunmehr einen eigenen EU-politischen Rahmen fuer Civil Defence. Die Jahrestagung markiert den Uebergang von Strategie-Launch zu Umsetzung. Fuer Hamburg relevant: **EU Stockpiling Network**, **Civil Protection Mechanism Revision** und **Crisis Coordination Hub** — alle bilden den europaeischen Bezugsrahmen fuer nationalen und laendischen Bevoelkerungsschutz.
