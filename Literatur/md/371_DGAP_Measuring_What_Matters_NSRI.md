# Measuring What Matters

**Publication Date:** February 18, 2026

**Authors:** Patrick Keller, Thomas Kleine-Brockhoff

**Organization:** German Council on Foreign Relations (DGAP) & PwC Germany

---

## Overview

This external publication introduces the National Security & Resilience Index (NSRI), a collaborative initiative between PwC's Defense & Security Institute and DGAP designed to quantify national security and resilience across multiple dimensions.

## Key Context

The authors emphasize that Germany faces a fundamental shift in its security posture, transitioning from security consumer to provider. The document states: *"From a consumer to a provider of existential security, for herself and the continent."*

## Core Framework

The NSRI measures three interconnected elements:

1. **Military Capability** – Armed forces strength and readiness
2. **Industrial Capacity** – Economic production and manufacturing resilience
3. **Societal Readiness** – Population stability and critical infrastructure robustness

## Central Insight

A critical finding highlights interdependence: *"The three major elements...are so closely interlinked that each will fail without the success of the other."* This underscores that military investment alone cannot ensure security without supporting economic and social infrastructure.

## Project Status

The authors characterize the NSRI as preliminary research requiring further development, inviting collaborative refinement under academic guidance.
