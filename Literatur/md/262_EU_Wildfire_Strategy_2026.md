# Commission launches new strategy to tackle rising wildfire threat

25 March 2026

The European Commission has unveiled a comprehensive integrated approach to wildfire risk management addressing prevention, preparedness, response, and recovery. This strategy aims to enhance Europe's resilience against escalating wildfire threats while protecting citizens, ecosystems, infrastructure, and cultural heritage.

## Current Crisis

Europe faced its worst wildfire season on record in 2025, with over one million hectares burned. Climate change intensifies both the size and frequency of these disasters, creating an urgent need for coordinated action across the continent.

## Prevention Focus

The Commission emphasizes ecosystem-based wildfire prevention, promoting fire-resilient landscapes through nature protection and restoration. A new guidance document on Natura 2000 sites provides structured approaches to climate adaptation while maintaining conservation objectives. The document clarifies flexibilities for Member States during emergency situations requiring rapid response.

Updated risk assessment guidelines will help Member States integrate long-term wildfire planning into national reporting systems.

## Preparedness and Citizen Engagement

According to recent European Environment Agency data, roughly half of surveyed citizens worry about wildfires. The Commission plans to increase public awareness through educational staff training, EU youth programs, and a dedicated European Citizens Panel.

## Enhanced Response Capabilities

The rescEU firefighting fleet will expand with 12 firefighting planes and 5 helicopters. Romania received the first helicopter in January 2026, ready for the upcoming season. A new European firefighting hub in Cyprus will serve dual functions — emergency response and capacity building.

## Technological Advancement

The Commission will develop standardized pan-European risk modeling and AI-assisted wildfire tools to support decision-making. The European Forest Fire Information System, supported by Copernicus satellites, will improve early warning and fire monitoring.

## Quotes

Teresa Ribiera (Executive Vice-President): "Strengthening our prevention and resilience capacities and investing in healthy ecosystems will help us to reduce the most dangerous risks."

Roxana Mînzatu (Executive Vice-President): "Europe must be ready before the first fire starts."

Hadja Lahbib (Commissioner): "Wildfires know no borders, and neither should our response."

Jessika Roswall (Commissioner): "By investing in prevention, restoring nature and creating fire-resilient landscapes, we can avoid economic damage worth billions of euros."

## Implementation

The Commission will present a proposal for a Council Recommendation on integrated wildfire risk management, working closely with Member States, regions, civil protection communities, and land managers to ensure coordinated, whole-of-society implementation. Progress will be regularly reviewed and reported to the European Parliament and other key institutions.
