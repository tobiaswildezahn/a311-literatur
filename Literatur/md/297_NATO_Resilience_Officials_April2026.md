# Senior National Officials for Resilience Meet at NATO to Address the Crucial Role of Resilience in Allied Security

**Date:** 28 April 2026
**Source:** NATO News
**URL:** https://nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2026/04/28/senior-national-officials-for-resilience-meet-at-nato-to-address-the-crucial-role-of-resilience-in-allied-security

## Article Text

On Tuesday, 28 April 2026, senior national officials for resilience convened at NATO headquarters to discuss advancements in resilience-related investments. The gathering brought together experts from all NATO member states to exchange perspectives and knowledge aimed at strengthening societal protection. According to NATO Deputy Secretary General Radmila Shekerinska, close collaboration among NATO, national militaries, and civilian authorities proves essential for preparing the Alliance against emerging risks and threats.

Member nations are making substantial financial commitments to fulfill the pledge established at The Hague summit regarding spending 5% of national GDP on core defense and security-related capabilities. These investments encompass infrastructure development, defense industry expansion, and resilience initiatives.

The meeting marked a historic milestone by bringing together senior resilience officials and national logistics directors in a joint session for the first time. This collaborative approach strengthens cooperation across government levels and throughout society while enhancing Host Nation Support delivery mechanisms. By combining civilian and military expertise, member states aim to bolster NATO's collective defense capabilities.

Deputy Chair of the Military Committee, Lieutenant General Winston P. Brooks, emphasized the connection between operational enablement and logistical support, noting alignment regarding the fundamental importance of Host Nation Support in NATO's defense strategy.

The gathering also resulted in launching an updated version of NATO's Baseline Requirements for Resilience, underscoring the Alliance's commitment to continuous improvement in preparedness.
