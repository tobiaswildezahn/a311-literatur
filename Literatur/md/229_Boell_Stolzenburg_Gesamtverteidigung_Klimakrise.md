# Comprehensive Defence in the Climate Crisis: Why Preparedness Shapes Resilience

**Author:** Kathrin Stolzenburg
**Date:** 21 January 2026
**Institution:** Heinrich Böll Stiftung
**License:** CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0

---

## Security Begins Before the Crisis

Contemporary security discussions across Germany and Europe have intensified considerably. Military conflicts, hybrid threats, vulnerable supply chains, and shifting geopolitical power dynamics characterize the current strategic context. The climate emergency simultaneously functions as a profound structural shift that magnifies and compounds these challenges. Under these circumstances, security and defence require understanding beyond exclusively military or reactive frameworks.

Climate change fundamentally alters peacetime state capacity conditions. Extreme weather phenomena—prolonged heat periods, water scarcity, intense precipitation, and uncontrolled fires—continuously stress government structures, response personnel, essential services, and distribution networks, frequently simultaneously and over extended timeframes. These pressures directly impact civilian institutions foundational to comprehensive defence, relocating security planning toward civilian infrastructure.

Climate science conclusively establishes that anthropogenic climate change raises frequencies and intensities of particular extreme phenomena. While not all weather events correlate with climate shifts, statistical evidence demonstrates robust relationships. Climate impacts remain worldwide, affecting developing and developed nations similarly, influencing migration movements, regional stability, commodity accessibility, and confrontational patterns.

> "The climate crisis exposes structural vulnerabilities within state and societal systems that are central to comprehensive defence."

A pivotal question emerges: Does climate change function predominantly as a threat amplifier for comprehensive defence—or does climate-adapted readiness represent strategic value, benefiting security, freedoms, and governance capacity?

---

## The Climate Crisis Is a Structural Risk Multiplier

Within security frameworks, climate transformation increasingly appears as a threat multiplication element. This evaluation holds validity but remains incomplete when examining discrete extreme occurrences exclusively. Fundamentally significant is its structural character: the climate emergency perpetually elevates baseline strain on government institutions.

Climate-triggered emergencies rarely materialize independently. They converge with concurrent pressures including disease outbreaks, fiscal disruption, or geopolitical friction. For emergency response and civil defence, identical personnel, structural, and equipment reserves face recurring simultaneous demands. Response teams execute parallel assignments, government bodies function under perpetual emergency conditions, and vital infrastructure endures continuous pressure. Sustained operational functioning and adaptive capacity under duress consequently become fundamental indicators of governance strength.

Security implications frequently receive inadequate consideration. Climate-based weaknesses permit deliberate exploitation through coordinated assaults, false narratives, or destabilization tactics below military engagement thresholds. Significant crisis circumstances therefore generate not merely physical but additionally political outcomes: they consume capacity, establish doubt, and potentially diminish institutional credibility.

Climate transformation intensifies precisely those ambiguous territories wherein conventional crisis legislation and protective frameworks demonstrate restricted usefulness. It illustrates that protection emerges not solely during emergency declarations, but throughout routine administration amid multifaceted, overlapping pressures.

---

## Emergency Responders, Municipalities, and the Economy: Sustainability of Operations as a Core Issue

Germany maintains an interconnected response architecture distributing functions across multiple organizational tiers and stakeholders. These elements function interdependently and reinforce one another. Operational continuity constitutes a fundamental consideration across all organizational levels—particularly for coordination and overall effectiveness.

This demonstrates particular clarity among emergency personnel engaged in emergency response services. Fire departments, medical response units, assistance organizations, and comparable crisis management bodies experience considerable strain from climate-triggered deployments. Intensified and more recurrent extreme phenomena necessitate prolonged mobilization, heightened mental and physical exhaustion, and actual workforce and facility damage.

> "The climate crisis thus intensifies precisely those grey zones in which traditional instruments of disaster law and defence planning have limited reach"

This remains critically significant for integrated defence frameworks. Civil defence mechanisms build upon peacetime crisis response capabilities. Emergency personnel remain accessible during escalated tensions or military situations exclusively if they function effectively amid adverse environmental circumstances. Operational persistence therefore transforms into a fundamental security element—technically, institutionally, and regarding personnel composition.

This demonstrates robust interconnection between municipal, regional, and federal governance, commercial enterprises, and aid services. Communities manage fundamental services, regulate critical systems, and function as primary community connections. Preparation choices—for instance alternative electrical supply, workforce allocation, contingency operations, or emergency information management—meaningfully shape operational capability. Commercial organizations similarly provide essential components: they regulate vital mechanisms, recruit crisis responders, and participate in neighbourhood distribution systems.

Resilient comprehensive defence materializes where participating entities coordinate preparedness strategies proactively, rather than initially during emergencies. Sustained climate-based stress renders this alignment simultaneously increasingly essential and increasingly apparent.

This broadened readiness understanding corresponds with Germany's National Resilience Strategy, designating resilience as perpetual collaborative responsibility encompassing governmental, corporate, and community participants.

## Preparedness Planning: Less Abstract, More Operational

Preparedness commonly receives equation with reserves or contingency documentation. From integrated security perspectives, this proves inadequate. Methodical readiness strategy aims not to predetermine every emergency circumstance comprehensively, but to preserve capacity for adaptive functioning amid uncertainty.

Functionally, this encompasses:

**First**, methodically charting interconnections. Electrical systems, aquatic provision, information technology, personnel, material transport, and data distribution form densely interrelated webs. Readiness investigation identifies vital intersections and knock-on consequences—such as power interruption promptly impairing medical care, administrative functions, and directive structures.

**Second**, establishing priorities preceding emergency circumstances. Which responsibilities require preservation regardless? Which temporary reductions remain manageable? These determinations involve political difficulty yet remain unavoidable—and without preparation, emergency conditions force rapid determinations amid severe limitations and intensive examination.

**Third**, guaranteeing organizational durability. This encompasses practical workforce allocation, redundancy in administrative hierarchy and messaging, and training mimicking constraints versus optimal scenarios.

**Fourth**, making cooperation operational. Readiness preparation establishes synchronized operations among regional entities, crisis services, commercial partners, and—where applicable—military elements. Personal relationships matter importantly, yet without organized frameworks they demonstrate fragility.

From this position, preparedness functions as the connector joining community durability and combat readiness. It augments protection without unduly constraining individual freedoms—particularly because it functions anticipatorily.

## Supply Systems and Resilience as the Foundation of Comprehensive Defence

One particularly delicate sector involves furnishing essential materials and functions. Electrical provision, aquatic accessibility, food production, medical services, transit, and information systems represent not merely ordinary conveniences; they constitute underpinnings of governmental legitimacy. Climate-triggered interruptions strike these mechanisms immediately, yielding repercussions comparable to protective strategy situations.

This presents a tactical opportunity: modifications fortifying climate-based durability of furnishing mechanisms concurrently reinforce comprehensive security competency. Durability constitutes not supplementary material, but a foundational framework. It prioritizes not comprehensive safeguarding of particular resources, but systemic competency to persist or modify amid strain.

The current assessment by the German Environment Agency emphasizes that resilience depends relatively little upon separate technical safeguarding systems versus clear authority structures, dependable information, and cooperative arrangements spanning communities, regional administration, governmental authorities, foundation operators, and crisis management personnel.

---

## A European Dimension: Resilience Has No Borders

Climate transformation recognizes no geographic limitations. Severe climate phenomena, manufacturing community disruptions, and materials constraints challenge continental Europe broadly. Integrated defence and durability consequently necessitate comprehension within continental frameworks—for instance, standardized readiness specifications, coordinated information evaluation, and reciprocal assistance throughout significant disruptions.

Civil response services offer especially promising cooperative channels beneath military collaboration. Climate-adapted readiness therefore reinforces both domestic and continental security constructs.

---

## Protecting Freedom Means Preparing in Advance

The climate transformation mandates recalibrated comprehension of protection—conceptualized not as extraordinary declaration, but as perpetual accountability. Autonomy and protection constitute complementary, not conflicting, principles. Durable democratic systems characterize themselves via capacity to navigate difficulties while preserving foundational standards.

Strategic readiness formulation meaningfully advances this objective throughout ordinary governance and critical circumstances. It generates operational capability, comprehensiveness, and legitimacy. Accordingly, climate transformation constitutes not marginal concern for integrated defence, but crucial examination: it demonstrates whether protection emerges methodically, collaboratively, and consistently with democratic values.
