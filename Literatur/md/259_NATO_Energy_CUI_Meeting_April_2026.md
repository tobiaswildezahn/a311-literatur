# NATO Meeting on Energy and Critical Undersea Infrastructure Security

**Date:** 17 April 2026

**Location:** NATO Headquarters, Brussels

## Meeting Overview

NATO Allies and industry representatives convened to address energy security and Critical Undersea Infrastructure (CUI) vulnerabilities in light of Middle East developments.

## Leadership

Ambassador Jean-Charles Ellermann-Kingombe, NATO's Assistant Secretary General for Cyber and Digital Transformation and Special Coordinator for Hybrid Threats, chaired the discussions.

## Participants

- NATO Allies representatives
- Industry representatives
- NATO staff members
- NATO Energy Security Centre of Excellence
- NATO's Allied Maritime Command

## Key Topics Discussed

Participants focused on:
- Energy vulnerabilities across NATO's operational area
- CUI security measures
- Potential security scenarios
- NATO's deterrence and defense capabilities

## Ambassador's Statement

Ellermann-Kingombe emphasized that energy security represents more than market considerations. She stated: *"Energy security is vital in ensuring our warfighting capacity"* and framed it as integral to NATO's core defense and deterrence missions.

## Context

The meeting underscored the interconnection between energy resilience, warfighting readiness, and NATO's broader security strategy in response to regional developments.
