# Operational Plan for Germany (OPLAN DEU)

## Overview

The Operational Plan for Germany represents a military cornerstone of the nation's comprehensive defence strategy. This classified document, spanning approximately 1,400 pages, integrates military and civilian resources across multiple government levels to address modern security challenges.

## Purpose and Rationale

Following Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine, Germany's security environment shifted dramatically. The plan addresses what officials describe as "the greatest threat to peace and security in Europe." It functions as a deterrence mechanism rather than offensive preparation, emphasizing prevention over conflict preparation.

The document reflects the nation's 2023 National Security Strategy, which frames defence as requiring "collaborative interaction of all relevant actors, resources and instruments" across society.

## Scope and Content

**Document Classification:** Designated as secret (GEHEIM); not publicly available for security reasons

**Development Timeline:**
- Initial version: Early 2024
- Current status: Living document with continuous updates (most recent in spring 2025)
- Next comprehensive revision: Mid-2026

**Leadership:** The Bundeswehr Joint Force Command in Berlin and Schwielowsee oversees implementation, with 16 regional territorial commands coordinating at state levels.

## Civil-Military Cooperation

A defining characteristic involves linking military operations with civilian support infrastructure across peacetime, hybrid threats, crisis, and wartime scenarios. This includes:

- Military assistance for civilian war preparedness
- Civilian contributions to defence planning
- Coordination between Bundeswehr and government entities
- Integration with federal states and municipalities

**Important Distinction:** The plan excludes non-military civilian preparedness outside Bundeswehr responsibility, such as essential services provision.

## Host Nation Support Requirements

Germany's central European location creates NATO obligations. The plan anticipates managing:

**Scale:** Up to 800,000 allied military personnel and 200,000 vehicles transiting Germany within six months during contingencies

**Support Categories:**
- Protection and security operations
- Traffic direction and transport management
- Accommodation and food services
- Refuelling and equipment maintenance
- Medical support and legal assistance
- Rail, road, seaport, and airport coordination

**Civilian Partnerships:** Agreements with companies including Deutsche Bahn, Rheinmetall, and Autobahn GmbH formalize commercial support frameworks.

## Key Military Elements

- **Cold-start capability:** Rapid force deployment readiness
- **Warfighting capacity:** Sustained operational effectiveness
- **Sustainability:** Long-term logistical support systems

## Relationship to Broader Defence Planning

**What OPLAN DEU Covers:** Military aspects and defence-critical infrastructure essential for armed forces operations

**What It Excludes:**
- General civil defence measures (Federal Ministry of Interior responsibility)
- Broader critical infrastructure protection
- Population protection from war effects
