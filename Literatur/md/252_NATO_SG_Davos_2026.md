# NATO Secretary General at World Economic Forum Davos 2026

**Date:** January 20-22, 2026
**Speaker:** NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte

## Key Talking Points

Rutte emphasized that "NATO is crucial, not only for the defence of Europe, but also for the defence of the United States."

He stressed Europe's safety depends on both internal efforts and strong transatlantic bonds.

## Defence Investment Emphasis

The Secretary General urged European allies to increase defence spending and called on industries to accelerate production and innovation in emerging technologies, particularly artificial intelligence and unmanned systems.

## Ukraine Support

Rutte reaffirmed commitment to Ukraine's defense, stating: "They need our support. It's also about our collective security, that's what is at stake here."

He expressed confidence in achieving a just and sustainable peace while maintaining ongoing military and humanitarian assistance to Ukraine.

## High-Level Engagements

Rutte met with President Trump regarding Arctic security, along with leaders from Canada, Montenegro, Norway, Japan, and U.S. Congressional delegations during the forum.
