# Partnering to Strengthen Resilience — NATO's Military Strategic Partnerships Conference 2026

**Date:** March 23–25, 2026
**Source:** SHAPE Public Affairs Office

## Event Overview

NATO's annual Military Strategic Partnerships Conference (MSPC) took place in Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina from March 23-25, 2026. The gathering assembled over 350 participants from 43 nations to discuss collective defense and international cooperation.

## Key Message

Rear Admiral Yusuf Karagulle, Deputy Chief of Staff of SHAPE's Partnerships Directorate, stressed that "NATO's relationship with its Partner Nations is critical for strengthening NATO's collective resilience" amid current geopolitical uncertainty. He emphasized the need for partnerships to remain adaptable in an evolving security landscape.

## Conference Topics

Discussions centered on international security trends, military approaches to resilience, civil preparedness, and defense capacity building.

## NATO's Partnership Network

NATO maintains formal partnerships with 35 non-member nations across multiple regions. The alliance and its partners represent approximately one-fifth of global territory and nearly two billion people. Partner nations contribute to NATO activities including policy development, defense capacity building, crisis prevention, and military operations.
