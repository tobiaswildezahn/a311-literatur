# Commission coordinates European response to Hantavirus outbreak

**Date:** 2026-05-11
**Publisher:** European Commission (DG ECHO)

## Overview

The European Commission is coordinating and supporting national authorities' response to a Hantavirus outbreak. The European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control assesses the risk to Europe's general population as "very low," provided infection prevention measures remain in place and considering hantaviruses do not spread easily between people.

## EU Civil Protection Mechanism provided safe evacuations

Following Spain's activation of the EU Civil Protection Mechanism on May 6, the Emergency Response Coordination Centre facilitated safe evacuation of passengers from the MV Hondius cruise ship. Five repatriation flights departed from Tenerife on May 10, coordinated and co-funded by the EU and executed by France, Spain, the Netherlands, Greece, and Ireland. A sixth final flight by the Netherlands completed the operation.

The Commission mobilized capabilities from its strategic reserve, including:
- A medical evacuation aircraft from the EU fleet (hosted by Norway)
- Pre-positioned equipment in Tenerife
- Additional transport, logistics, and protective equipment

A Liaison Officer was deployed to Tenerife for on-site coordination. Two ECDC experts from the EU Health Task Force were deployed aboard the ship before disembarkation.

## Facilitating a common approach across Member States and global partners

The Commission supports the health response through collaboration with Spain, Member States, ECDC, the World Health Organization, and G7 partners.

The Health Security Committee, chaired by the Commission, aligns measures for safe disembarkation, repatriation, and passenger follow-up. Daily coordination meetings occur involving the EU Civil Protection Mechanism and Health Security Committee to share information and adjust operational plans.

## Commissioner statements

Commissioner Hadja Lahbib emphasized: "Health threats can easily cross borders and that is why coordination is key."

Commissioner Olivér Várhelyi stated the Commission remains "ready to support Member States" while maintaining vigilance and coordination with ECDC and international partners.

## Background

Hantaviruses were included in the 2025 Health threat prioritisation assessment, with the Commission funding medical countermeasures development. The 2022 revised Regulation on serious cross-border health threats provides the legal framework for prevention, preparedness, surveillance, and response capabilities.
