# Commission invites 150 EU citizens to help shape future preparedness actions

**News article | 19 March 2026 | Directorate-General for European Civil Protection and Humanitarian Aid Operations (ECHO)**

## Event Details

Starting tomorrow, the 1st session of the European Citizens' Panel on Preparedness will convene in Brussels through Sunday. The initiative brings together „150 randomly selected citizens from across Europe" to develop recommendations for implementing the Preparedness Union Strategy. Commissioner Hadja Lahbib will officially open proceedings on Friday.

## Strategy Implementation Progress

One year following the adoption of the Preparedness Strategy, the Commission has advanced several key initiatives:

- **EU Stockpiling and Medical Countermeasures Strategies** ensure essential goods and medical supplies are available during crises
- A new **EU Stockpiling Network** enables Member States to coordinate assistance and distribute resources during emergencies
- **EU Civil Protection Mechanism revision** strengthens coordinated emergency response and the Emergency Response Coordination Centre
- The **EU Crisis Coordination Hub** consolidates existing rescEU reserves
- A new **Water Resilience Strategy** aims to enhance water security

## Citizen Preparedness Efforts

The EU and Member States are implementing awareness campaigns and providing guidance for **„72-hour household self-sufficiency"**, alongside integrating preparedness education into school curricula for early childhood and primary education.

## Conference Schedule

This is the 8th Citizens' Panel since their 2022 launch. Commissioner Lahbib and three panel participants will attend the EU Preparedness Conference on 23-24 March, bringing together public authorities, academia, private sector, and civil society stakeholders.

Plenary sessions will be streamed on the Citizens' Engagement Platform for public participation.

## Einordnung (A311)

Die Citizens Panels sind Instrument deliberativer Demokratie innerhalb der Conference on the Future of Europe-Nachfolgeformate. Ihre Uebertragung auf Bevoelkerungsschutz ist politisch neu und signalisiert, dass die Kommission gesellschaftliche Legitimation fuer moeglicherweise einschneidende Preparedness-Massnahmen (Stockpiling-Pflichten, Schutzbau-Normen, Curricula) sucht.
