# NATO Militaries Strengthening Allied Medical Resilience

**Date:** 07 April 2026
**Source:** NATO SHAPE

## Overview

NATO Allies conducted multiple major exercises during winter to enhance medical resilience and strengthen civil-military coordination across the Alliance. These training operations focus on delivering emergency medical care at scale during crises or conflicts.

## Leadership Perspective

Brigadier General Petter Iversen, Chair of the Committee of Chiefs of Military Medical Services in NATO (COMEDS), emphasized that medical support represents "a core element of Allied deterrence and defence." Reflecting on lessons from Russia's invasion of Ukraine, Iversen stressed that large-scale warfare significantly impacts both military personnel and civilian populations. He advocated for a comprehensive approach: "Health Security is Collective Security."

## Major Exercises Conducted

**Cold Response (Norway):**
Arctic-based exercise involving civilian and military personnel testing wartime health readiness, including movement of wounded across borders and distances to hospitals.

**Medic Quadriga 26 (Germany):**
Focused on the complete medical evacuation chain from front-line operations to strategic aeromedical evacuation, with civilian healthcare provider coordination. Largest and most complex medical service exercise conducted by the Bundeswehr in decades. Conducted February 16 to March 20 as part of the "Quadriga 2026" exercise cluster.

**Casualty Move 26 (Germany):**
Led by the Multinational Medical Coordination Centre — Europe, this exercise trained personnel in managing large patient volumes, medical command structures, and civil-military collaboration during major crises.
